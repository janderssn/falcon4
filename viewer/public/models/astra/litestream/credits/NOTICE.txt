Historical Context:
Wills Wing was founded in 1973 by Chris and Bob Wills as Sport Kites, Inc. Since
1977, the company was managed by Rob Kells, Michael W. Meier, Linda Meier, and Steven
Pearson as managing partners and shareholders. Chris Wills remained a shareholder
and Board of Directors member throughout the company's history.

These designs represent hang glider models produced
by Wills Wing between 1973 and 2025. They are being released as open hardware to
contribute to the advancement of hang gliding knowledge and safety.

Design Heritage:
- These designs evolved from earlier work in hang glider development
- They incorporate decades of testing, refinement, and real-world experience
- Most of these designs were certified under HGMA (Hang Glider Manufacturers
  Association) standards of their time
- Many of these designs were also certified to LTF-2009 (DHV) airworthiness standards

Additional Notices:
1. These designs may not meet current certification standards and are provided
   primarily for historical, educational, and research purposes.
2. Any modification of these designs may affect their structural integrity,
   flight characteristics, and safety aspects.
3. Professional expertise is required to properly interpret and implement these
   designs.
4. See SAFETY.md for critical safety information.
