## Primary Disclaimer and Warning

Hang gliding is a form of aviation. Like any form of aviation, its safe practice demands the consistent
exercise of pilot skill, knowledge of airmanship and weather, judgment and attention at a level that is appropriate to the demands of each individual situation. Pilots who do not possess or exercise the required
knowledge, skills and judgment are frequently injured and killed. The statistical rate at which fatalities
occur in hang gliding is approximately one per thousand participants per year.

The Federal Aviation Administration does not require a pilot's license to operate a hang glider. Hang gliders and hang gliding equipment are not designed, manufactured, tested or certified to any state or federal
government airworthiness standards or requirements. Federal Aviation Regulation Part 103 states in part,
"ultralight vehicles are not required meet the airworthiness certification standards specified for aircraft
or to have certificates of airworthiness" and "operators of ultralight vehicles are not required to meet any
aeronautical knowledge, age, or experience requirements to operate those vehicles or to have airman or
medical certificates."

These designs were previously manufactured by Wills Wing but are now released as open source hardware. Neither the original Wills Wing designs nor any derivatives are covered by product liability insurance.

As a hang glider pilot, you are entirely responsible for your own safety. You should never attempt to fly a
hang glider without having received competent instruction. We strongly recommend that you not participate in hang gliding unless you recognize fully and wish to personally assume all of the associated risks.

## Additional Safety Warnings for Open Source Hardware Implementation

### Design and Construction Risks

1. These designs are historical and may not meet current certification standards
2. Any modification to these designs may affect:
   - Structural integrity
   - Flight characteristics
   - Stability
   - Control response
   - Safety margins
3. Manufacturing processes are critical to safety
4. Material specifications must be strictly followed
5. Quality control during construction is essential

### Required Expertise

Construction or modification of these designs requires:
- Expert knowledge of hang glider design principles
- Understanding of aerodynamics and structural engineering
- Experience with aerospace-grade materials and construction techniques
- Ability to perform proper testing and verification
- Knowledge of applicable standards and best practices

### Safety-Critical Components

Special attention must be paid to:
1. Airframe structural components
2. Control systems
3. Hardware and fasteners
4. Sail construction and attachment
5. Cable systems and terminations

### Required Testing and Verification

Any constructed or modified design must undergo:
1. Complete structural analysis
2. Material testing and verification
3. Static load testing
4. Flight testing by qualified test pilots
5. Independent safety review

### Certification and Standards

While not legally required, constructed gliders should meet:
1. HGMA (Hang Glider Manufacturers Association) standards where applicable
2. Current industry best practices
3. Local regulatory requirements
4. Applicable airworthiness standards

### Documentation Requirements

Maintain detailed records of:
1. All materials used
2. Construction processes
3. Testing procedures and results
4. Modifications from original design
5. Flight testing results

### Pre-Flight Safety

1. Complete thorough pre-flight inspections
2. Maintain proper maintenance records
3. Regular structural inspections
4. Component lifetime tracking
5. Proper setup and breakdown procedures

## Emergency Procedures

Document and understand:
1. Emergency procedures specific to the design
2. Recovery techniques
3. Safety limitations
4. Weather limitations
5. Flight envelope restrictions

## Recommended Safety Resources

1. Review product-specific safety information and technical bulletins at [willswing.com](https://www.willswing.com)
2. Contact your national hang gliding organization
3. Seek professional instruction
4. Join a certified hang gliding club
5. Obtain proper training and certification
6. Maintain current knowledge of safety practices

## Historical Context

These designs represent hang gliders that were previously manufactured professionally. Home-built versions may not achieve the same level of consistency or quality as factory-built versions. All modifications should be approached with extreme caution.

## Reporting Safety Issues

If you discover any safety-critical issues with these designs, immediately:
1. Document the issue
2. Create an issue in this repository
3. Contact relevant hang gliding safety organizations
4. Notify others who may be using these designs

## Final Warning

Please fly safely. If you have any doubt about your ability to safely construct, modify, or fly these designs, do not proceed. The risks of hang gliding are substantial and require proper training, equipment, and judgment.
