# Litestream control frame

Original hardware design copyright 1973–2026 Steven Pearson and Michael W. Meier,
formerly Sport Kites, Inc. dba Wills Wing. The original design files and notices
are available from the [Wills Wing hardware repository](https://github.com/Pearson00/WillsWing).

For the Falcon 4 viewer, the Astra model converts the component designs to triangle
meshes, positions the handed fittings and skids, reconstructs the uprights and
speedbar, and compresses the assembly for browser display. This version was
prepared on 22 November 2026. [Astra configuration](https://github.com/janderssn/falcon4-astra/issues/7) ·
[Original hardware revision](https://github.com/avishalom/WillsWing/tree/e09e6fca1d541e8974b68d753862e9572c963b3d)
